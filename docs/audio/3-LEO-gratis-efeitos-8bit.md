# 8-bit interface effects production brief

Keep the interface cues short, soft enough for children, and consistent in volume. The asset tree includes WAV effects generated by [the synthesis script](../../scripts/gerar-efeitos-8bit.py). The player also accepts MP3 equivalents. [Runtime audio guide](../SONS-E-VOZES.md)

For manual alternatives, use a preset in [jsfxr](https://sfxr.me/) or [ChipTone](https://sfbgames.itch.io/chiptone), then adjust duration, pitch, and pitch slide to fit the cue. Export WAV and keep the base filenames.

| Cue under `sfx/` | Where it plays | Preset and direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clique`, `clique_agudo`, `clique_grave` | General click, power up, power down | Blip/Select with medium, high, and low pitches |
| `confirmar`, `pronto` | Confirm an option or an energy plan | Two short rising notes or a short Powerup |
| `alerta`, `urgente` | Map alert or urgent event | Two soft beeps or alternating notes without harsh treble |
| `abrir_carta`, `abrir_menu` | Open card or menu | Short rising or soft Blip/Select |
| `custo`, `ganho` | Resource loss or gain | Low Hit/Hurt or short bright Pickup/Coin |
| `ampulheta` | Deferred cost arrives | Short ticks and a lower note |
| `aviso_rovi`, `rovi_fala` | Resource warning or typing | Two descending beeps or quiet 30 ms ticks |
| `estrela`, `figurinha` | Star or new sticker | Bright Pickup/Coin or a soft sparkle |
| `bom_dia`, `dormir` | Morning or bedtime | Rising square-wave or falling triangle-wave jingle |
| `sem_energia` | Insufficient power | Short low buzz |

Suggested jingle notes are C5 E5 G5 C6 for morning and G5 E5 C5 G4 for bedtime. Review loudness and file names before adding finished sounds to `public/audio/sfx/`.
